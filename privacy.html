Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Purpose

iCloud Calendar Sync is a personal, non-commercial application used by its owner to synchronise calendar appointments from Apple iCloud Calendar to a personal Google Calendar.

Information processed

The application processes calendar event titles, dates, times, descriptions, locations and identifiers necessary for synchronisation.

How information is used

Calendar information is used solely to create, update and reconcile appointments in the owner's personal Google Calendar. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Storage and third-party services

The application runs using GitHub Actions and communicates with Apple's iCloud CalDAV service and the Google Calendar API.

Authentication credentials are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets. Synchronised appointments and synchronisation metadata are stored in Google Calendar. Technical execution logs may be retained by GitHub.

Sharing and retention

The resulting personal Google Calendar may be shared with accounts explicitly authorised by the owner. Event information remains until removed by the owner or through the application's synchronisation process.

Access and deletion

The owner can stop synchronisation, revoke application access through their Google and Apple accounts, and delete synchronised calendar appointments.
